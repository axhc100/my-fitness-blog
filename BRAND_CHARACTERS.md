# FitKit Brand Visual Identity: Official Characters

All future FitKit article illustrations and visual media **MUST** feature one of the following two official brand characters (alternating between them).

---

## Character 1: Western Blonde Bob Athlete
- **Ethnicity & Age**: Young Caucasian female athlete in her early 20s.
- **Hair**: Sleek platinum blonde / pale ash blonde short bob haircut, chin-length, neat and elegant with gentle volume.
- **Face**: Delicate symmetrical European facial features, straight nose, warm hazel/light-brown eyes, fair clean complexion.
- **Physique**: Lean, athletic, toned shoulders and core, graceful clavicles, natural aesthetic athletic posture.
- **Attire**: Minimalist sleek black athletic fitness sports top / sports bra and black training leggings/shorts.

## Character 2: Asian Blonde Bob Athlete
- **Ethnicity & Age**: Young East Asian female athlete in her early 20s.

## Visual Consistency Rules for Image Generation
1. **Aspect Ratio**: Always 16:9 widescreen landscape for article banners.
2. **Photography Style**: High-end commercial sports photography, natural gym or outdoor lighting, sharp focus on the character and sports action, shallow depth of field in background, 8k resolution.
3. **Anatomical & Biomechanical Rigor**:
   - Natural hands with 5 fingers, authentic grip on sports equipment (dumbbells, barbells, mats, straps).
   - Biomechanically accurate movement postures matching the article topic.
4. **Cleanliness**: Absolutely NO text overlays, NO logos/watermarks, NO anatomical distortion.
