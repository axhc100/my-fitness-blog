# FitKit Brand Visual Identity: Official Characters

All future FitKit article illustrations and visual media **MUST** feature one of the following two official brand characters (alternating between them).

---

## Core Brand Rule: Facial Constancy & Scene Flexibility (五官锁死，发型/发色/服装百变)
- **严格保持不变（Strictly Constant）**：
  - **面部五官与骨相（Facial Features & Identity）**：必须严格继承基准参考图的五官特征、眼型、鼻梁轮廓、唇形与面部比例，保持同一人物的真实辨识度。
- **允许自由更换（Freely Variable per Scene）**：
  - **发型与发色（Hairstyle & Hair Color）**：可根据运动场景自由变换（例如：户外跑步的高马尾、瑜伽普拉提的丸子头或麻花辫、力量房的利落短发或微卷发；黑发、棕发、金发、亚麻灰等自然发色均可）。
  - **服装与穿搭（Apparel & Sportswear）**：契合文章具体的运动主题进行多样化时尚搭配（例如：莫兰迪色瑜伽套装、透气跑步风衣、高强度训练短裤背心、运动护膝/手套、冷色系机能风运动服等）。

---

## Character 1: Official Western Athlete (欧美风官方主角)
- **Identity & Demographics**: Young Caucasian female athlete in her early 20s.
- **Constant Facial Structure (五官保持不变)**: Delicate symmetrical European facial features, elegant straight nose bridge, warm hazel/light-brown eyes, subtle natural facial contours and expression.
- **Physique**: Lean, athletic, toned shoulders and core, natural aesthetic athletic posture.
- **Variable Elements (可变)**: Hairstyle (high ponytail, braids, bun, or bob), hair color, sports attire appropriate to the exercise.

## Visual Quality Standards
1. **Aspect Ratio**: Always 16:9 widescreen landscape for article banners.
2. **Photography Style**: High-end commercial sports photography, authentic gym/outdoor lighting, sharp focus on the character and action, shallow depth of field in background, 8k resolution.
3. **Anatomical & Biomechanical Rigor**:
   - Natural hands with 5 fingers, realistic grip on sports gear.
   - Scientifically accurate movement postures matching the article topic.
4. **Cleanliness**: Absolutely NO text overlays, NO logos/watermarks, NO anatomical distortion.
